The Real Problem

Same commute. Rising friction.

"From college gate to home — a long, uncertain and costly journey."

1. College Ends

Classes over, rush outside

2. Search

Opens apps / looks around

3. Wait

10-20 min average wait

4. Check Price & Negotiate

Surge pricing & bargaining (Peak Friction)

5. Travel

Reaches home, exhausted

Why does this keep happening? (5 Whys)

1. Why is the ride costly?
Because there are very few passengers paying the full fare.
2. Why few passengers?
There is no way to find people going in the exact same direction.
3. Why is there no way?
Current apps are car-centric, not built for auto-sharing at a micro-level.
4. Why not solved yet?
There is no real-time, dynamic route matching optimized for autos.
Root Cause
Lack of a simple, trusted, student-friendly shared auto ecosystem.

The Solution

Introducing SAATHCHALO
