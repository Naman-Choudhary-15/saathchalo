SAATHCHALO Privacy Policy

Zero data exploitation • Full commuter privacy guarantee

1. Commuter Identity & Community Visibility

When you participate in SAATHCHALO regional communities, only your display name and selected avatar are visible to fellow commuters in chat, destination voting, and shared rides. Your email address, phone number, and password hashes are strictly protected and never exposed.

2. Location Privacy & Live Social Mobility Map

Live GPS location sharing on the Social Mobility Map is strictly opt-in. When enabled, your approximate position is broadcast to active community peers to facilitate trajectory pooling. You can toggle Location Visibility OFF at any moment in the Live Map controls to become completely invisible on the map while retaining community access.

3. Pricing Privacy

SAATHCHALO calculates equitable fare distribution using proportional distance ratios. Customers see only the verified, vehicle-specific estimated fare for their commute. Internal rates, markup models, and allocation thresholds are kept confidential to ensure fair, transparent consumer pricing.

4. Permanent Account Deletion

Under Google Play and GDPR standards, you retain complete ownership of your data. You may delete your account, session tokens, and all associated profile records at any time directly inside Profile → Delete Account.

Community Guidelines & Terms

Rules for safe, respectful, and reliable shared transit

1. Respectful Communication

Community chat is intended for route coordination, finding travel companions, and sharing transit updates. Harassment, hate speech, spam, and unsolicited commercial activity will lead to immediate account suspension.

2. Reward Points & Reliability Policy on Confirmed Rides

Community destination voting coordinates shared vehicles for groups of riders. If you vote, confirm participation in an arranged shared ride, and subsequently fail to show up without timely cancellation, 5 reward points may be deducted from your SAATHCHALO reliability balance to protect vehicle drivers and fellow commuters from empty reserved seats. Showing up or cancelling before the cutoff incurs 0 deduction, and maintaining a healthy balance unlocks future perks.

3. Content Moderation & Reporting

Any message or behavior violating community safety can be reported instantly using the flag icon next to messages. Reports are immediately logged for administrative and safety review.
